Night of the Hollow Moon

Speaking lesson, A1. We talk about Halloween, our stuff and our stories. Tap the dashed phrases to build your answer, then say it out loud.

Wh-word + have / has + subject + got…? → What have you got? What has he got?
Wh-word + am / is / are + subject…? → Where are you? Who is she?
Short answers: I’ve got… / He’s got… / I haven’t got… / She hasn’t got…
